Local-First Architecture

Take complete command of your personal finances in absolute privacy. FinanceFlow combines the power of encrypted local storage with on-device Apple Intelligence and end-to-end encrypted iCloud sync. Pay once ($9.99 USD), own it forever—no monthly fees, no third-party tracking, and no external servers.

Core Capabilities

1. Complete Privacy & Security

Hardware-accelerated SQLCipher (AES-256) encryption keeps your transactions, balances, and accounts private. Unlocks via Face ID, Touch ID, Quick PIN, or Master Password.

2. End-to-End Encrypted iCloud Sync

Your encrypted vault syncs seamlessly across iPhone, iPad, and Mac via Apple's CloudKit private database. All data leaves your device encrypted with AES-256-GCM—unreadable to Apple and anyone else.

3. Cash Flow Forecasting

Look ahead 30 to 365 days into the future. Interactive 35-day calendar grid, opening balance anchor, scheduled bills, and upcoming income projections prevent surprises before they occur.

4. Multi-Format Statement Import

Import bank statements and spreadsheets in CSV, Excel (.xlsx), and PDF formats. Snap receipts with Continuity Camera and parse them instantly on-device with Vision OCR.

5. On-Device Apple Intelligence

Daily AI spending insights, anomaly alerts, natural language ledger search, and private voice entry powered 100% locally by Apple FoundationModels and Speech.

6. Envelopes, Goals & Subscriptions

Flexible envelope budgeting with clear unbudgeted spend separation, automated subscription radar with renewal alerts, and milestone savings trackers.
